About SIDcog Demo

This is a browser port of P1 SIDcog v1.4, the January 2021 revision by Johannes Ahlebrand and Ada Gottensträter. One AudioWorklet runs the three voices, envelopes, combined waveform table, fixed-point filter, and mixer. It converts the original dual-mono duty value to PCM and resamples the 30,792.918 Hz synthesis output for your audio device.

The reference description assigns six of the Propeller P1’s eight cogs to sound, composite video, two graphics tasks, SD access, and control. Here, the AudioWorklet consumes a timed 25-byte register stream. The Canvas display follows its playback position and draws the waveform from its PCM.

The black introduction, blue display, bitmap lettering, and scrolling text are reconstructed from the supplied scene descriptions. The music chapter plays the supplied 013.dmp, which the Parallax forum identifies as Ahle’s SIDcog demo dump. Playback starts at the first music block by default. Register dump playback settings offer the recorded prelude and the forum’s other frame rates. The track menu also includes Lost Friend, the example register dump from Johannes Ahlebrand’s Parallax OBEX package, played at the example’s 50 Hz rate.

The observatory artwork was created with GPT Image for this player. The register panel shows each voice’s seven bytes, with brief highlights when bits change. The miniature plots show the actual amplitude envelopes sampled from the synthesizer. The activity display has one horizontal lane per register, with new changes entering from the right. The frame counter uses zero-based dump positions.

The port retains the v1.4 ADSR approximation, its combined-wave quirks, and its filter coefficient decoding. The original SID’s ADSR timing bug is absent from this engine.
